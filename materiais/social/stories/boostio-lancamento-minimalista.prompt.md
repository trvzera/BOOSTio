# Story minimalista da Boostio

Método: ferramenta integrada de geração/edição de imagens.

Edit the attached BOOSTio launch Instagram Story into a radically simpler minimalist editorial Story. Keep the brand identity and blue #007BFF, off-white and near-black palette. Output one finished vertical 9:16 Story, ideally 1080x1920 or higher, no mockup or social UI.

Create a perfectly flat solid near-black #101114 background. Remove the PC photograph, floor, lighting effects, arc, textures and all existing text except the brand logo. Faithfully retain the exact recognizable BOOSTio wordmark with the pill toggle replacing the double O, its knob on the right, and correct B / toggle / ST / io proportions. Scale the logo smaller, around 25% of the canvas width, upper-left within safe margins. This is an existing logo, not a redesign.

Use generous intentional negative space and a restrained left-aligned typographic composition. At least 60% of the canvas should be visually empty. Consistent 10% side margins, important content at least 12% from top and 17% from bottom. Clean geometric sans-serif, Lexend-like, medium/semibold rather than very heavy all-caps. Precise elegant line spacing.

Main headline around the vertical middle, exact Portuguese text:
"A Boostio
chegou."
First line off-white, second line brand blue. Large enough for immediate legibility but not oversized to fill the frame.

Below with generous spacing, exact supporting text in smaller muted light gray:
"Menos dúvidas,
mais desempenho."

Near the lower safe area, a small understated blue arrow and exact white call to action:
"Conheça a plataforma ↓"

Leave a purposeful empty zone below the CTA, still above the bottom UI safe area, for the user to insert a native Instagram link sticker. Do not draw a fake button, sticker or placeholder. Do not add a switch illustration because the switch is already in the logo. No extra labels, no borders, no lines, no icons except the single arrow in the CTA, no additional slogans, no gradients, no shadows, no imagery, no gaming neon. This should feel like a calm, beautifully typeset premium brand launch announcement, extremely simple and intentional. Correct accents and ç. No watermarks.
