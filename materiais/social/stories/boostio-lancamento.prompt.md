# Story de lançamento da Boostio

Método: ferramenta integrada de geração/edição de imagens.

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create ONE finished launch Instagram Story for BOOSTio, the Brazilian PC planning platform. Portrait 9:16 canvas, ideally 1080x1920 or higher in the exact same ratio. Full-bleed finished graphic artwork, no mockup, no Instagram UI, no surrounding border.

Input image 1: the approved PC scene, the photographic hero and lighting reference. Preserve the same believable dark PC tower identity, side-window details, three-quarter viewing angle, charcoal case, understated royal blue rim light and moody studio floor. Recompose it elegantly in the lower-middle section for a tall Story.
Input image 2: the OFFICIAL logo, which is an identity-preservation insert, not an invitation to redesign it. Faithfully preserve the distinctive uppercase B, the single blue pill-shaped toggle replacing the double O with circular blue knob on the RIGHT, the uppercase S and T, and lowercase blue io. Keep original letter shapes and spacing. Place the wordmark modestly in the upper-left safe area. No alternate font or invented logo shape.

Art direction: match a sophisticated editorial hardware brand launch, sparse and impactful. Near-black #08090B and charcoal background, white type, blue #007BFF accent, natural subtle photographic depth and floor highlights. Typographic hierarchy and composition inspired by a contemporary graphic design studio, using approachable Lexend-like bold geometric sans headlines and Poppins-like clean body text. No gamer neon, RGB rainbows, excessive gradients, tech circuitry, floating interface cards, 3D slogans or stock-template clutter. Plenty of breathing room. One very thin curved pale-blue editorial line may frame the PC, but keep it understated.

Story-safe composition: important text and logo at least 9% from left/right edges, at least 10% from top and 14% from bottom. Keep everything critical readable at phone size. Do not crowd the composition.

Exact text only:
Small understated blue-accent uppercase label near the logo: "LANÇAMENTO"
Large main headline in upper-middle, left-aligned with strong readable linebreaks:
"SEU PRÓXIMO PC
COMEÇA AQUI."
Make "COMEÇA AQUI." vivid royal blue and the first phrase white; large bold typography, no awkward hyphenation. Correct Portuguese accents.
Short supporting text below the headline:
"Planeje seu primeiro PC ou próximo upgrade com a Boostio."
Break naturally into two or three clean short lines, smaller light gray type.
Small brand tagline in lower safe section:
"Menos dúvidas, mais desempenho."
Simple clear call to action below the tagline:
"CONHEÇA A BOOSTio ↓"

The PC should be a strong beautiful photographic hero in the middle/lower part, integrated naturally into the dark scene. Keep the headline completely unobstructed. Place supporting text and footer where their contrast remains excellent. Beneath the CTA leave a small purposeful EMPTY SPACE within the safe central lower region for the user to add a native Instagram link sticker. Do NOT draw a fake sticker, a URL, QR code, button, app interface, user handle or placeholder instructions. Final graphic should immediately feel like the launch of the actual BOOSTio brand and invite the viewer to visit the platform. No new claims of performance, prices, discounts or automatic AI features. No watermarks.
