# Story de lançamento — linguagem dos posts

Método: ferramenta integrada de geração/edição de imagens.

Create a new BOOSTio launch Instagram Story that closely matches the editorial language of the attached APPROVED 3x3 POST GRID. The previous minimalist Story was too bare: this version needs purposeful visual composition and hardware imagery while staying clean. Output ONE full-bleed portrait 9:16 image, ideally 1080x1920 or higher, no grid, no mockup, no app chrome.

Reference 1: approved feed designs. Use the bold geometric typography, photographic hardware CUTOUTS, precise thin outline graphics, compact rounded pill accents, and black/blue/off-white palette. Pay special attention to the top-center typography poster, middle-left PC with arc, and bottom-center outlined pills. This should feel like another post from that brand, adapted into one tall Story.
Reference 2: exact OFFICIAL LOGO. Preserve its distinctive B / single pill toggle replacing the double O / S T / lowercase io structure, original shapes and spacing, blue knob on the right, white letters on black. Small logo at upper-left safe area, no redesign.
Reference 3: the approved PC hardware asset. Preserve that dark case identity and viewing angle, but use it as a clean editorial cutout, not an enormous cinematic photograph.

ART DIRECTION:
Flat near-black #101114 background, off-white #F1F1EF and saturated blue #007BFF. Clean contemporary graphic design studio approach, composed rather than sparse to the point of emptiness. Plenty of breathing room but strong visual rhythm. No textured floor, no dramatic blue atmosphere, no neon, no busy diagrams, no gradients across the canvas, no excessive decorative elements. Geometric Lexend-like sans serif headings, clean Poppins-like support text. Beautiful consistent margins.

LAYOUT:
Keep important content at least 9% from sides, 11% from top, 16% from bottom.
Header: small official logo left; at right a tiny fine rounded outlined pill containing exactly "LANÇAMENTO".
Upper third: strong left-aligned headline, exact words:
"SEU SETUP
MERECE UM
BOOST."
Off-white type, blue emphasis. Put "BOOST." in off-white on one precise saturated blue rectangular highlight strip, like the typographic treatment in the approved grid. Distinctive editorial hierarchy; readable without filling every available inch.
Immediately below headline, modest supporting copy, exactly:
"Planeje seu PC ou upgrade."

Middle/lower-middle: a moderately sized realistic dark PC case cutout in three-quarter perspective, occupying about 35% of the entire Story height. The case is centered slightly right. Behind it one large flat solid blue circle partly hidden by the case, plus ONE thin off-white elliptical arc extending around it, like a restrained editorial collage. Keep the tower wholly separated from headline text, no overlapping or unreadable copy. Match the clean dark hardware and natural studio highlights in reference 3. No floor photo or environment. A tiny soft contact shadow is okay, no neon glow.
Add two small off-white rounded outlined pills alongside the case in the negative space, exact words "Primeiro PC" and "Upgrade". These are editorial labels, not UI buttons. Make them small and carefully positioned, not random floating cards.

Lower safe area: a short thin off-white rule above modest tagline, exact:
"Menos dúvidas, mais desempenho."
Then a clear but understated call to action, exact:
"Conheça a Boostio ↓"
Leave a usable small empty zone BELOW the CTA within the lower central safe area for a native Instagram link sticker added by the user later. Do not draw a fake sticker, fake button, placeholder or URL.

FINISH:
A polished balanced brand Story that looks like the approved feed posts: a bold typographic poster plus purposeful hardware collage. More expressive than a plain text announcement, more restrained than a full cinematic PC advertisement. Only the specified text, correct Portuguese accents and ç, exact brand identity, no numbers or performance claims, no watermarks.
